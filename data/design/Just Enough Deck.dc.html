The problem

Two mistakes are possible. They do not cost the same.

Order too little and you lose a sale. Order too much and you throw stock away. Treating those as equally bad is what makes a full shelf feel like caution.

Order too little
You lose the margin

On a 30% margin you are out 30% of the price. The stock cost you nothing, because you never bought it.

Order too much
You lose most of the unit

You paid 70% of the price and threw most of it away, plus the cost of holding it while it sat there.
